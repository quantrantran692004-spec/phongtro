# HUONGMUOI · Trọ Thái Nguyên

## Direction

Contemporary local rental discovery: clear, direct, practical and distinct through real Thái Nguyên context. Keep the supplied HUONGMUOI wordmark and identify the service as Trọ Thái Nguyên. The product should feel current and approachable, not like a hotel, editorial magazine, or generic real-estate template.

## Tokens

- Canvas: white `#FFFFFF`; alternate section: slate `#F8FAFC`; cards: white.
- Main text: `#0F172A`; supporting text: `#475569`; borders: `#E2E8F0`.
- Brand accent: teal `#0F766E`; hover `#115E59`; light accent `#F0FDFA`.
- Typography: Be Vietnam Pro / Plus Jakarta Sans. Sans-serif throughout; no decorative serif display.
- Cards: 14–16px radius, thin border, near-flat rest state, small 2px lift and restrained shadow on hover.
- Motion: 150–250ms for controls. Hero image reveal is brief; disable motion when reduced motion is requested.

## Layout and content

- Keep the proven search-first home flow: local message, a truthful room photo from an actual listing, then quick category and filter controls.
- Preserve renter, landlord, map, comparison, booking, account and admin behavior.
- Show prices, room counts, availability, areas, distances and verification only when sourced from records. The current product uses demo/local-storage data; do not imply a live inventory.
- Keep direct contact visible: Trần Minh Quân · 0357041593.
- Map: OpenStreetMap tiles with visible attribution, quiet grayscale base, teal rent-price pills, amber university points, and DOM-built popup content.
- Listing cards prioritize room photo, rent, area, ward and utilities. Hover is subtle; image controls remain reachable on touch and keyboard.

## Avoid

Warm paper, sepia, brass/terracotta styling, oversized serif headlines, heavy gradients or vignettes, thick floating shadows, fake social proof, and markers or labels that collide.
