# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students attending universities in Thái Nguyên and engineers or workers at nearby industrial parks who need to find a room. Landlords who want to list and manage rooms directly.

## Product Purpose

Help renters discover available rooms in Thái Nguyên, understand their monthly and move-in costs, compare options, and contact landlords directly. Success means renters can make an informed shortlist and arrange a viewing.

## Positioning

A local room discovery service focused on direct landlord connections and transparent rental costs, including utilities and deposits.

## Operating Context

Renters search by neighborhood, university, price, room type, amenities, and map location. They may save, compare, calculate move-in costs, schedule visits, and report inaccurate listings. Landlords manage listings and appointments. Administrators review listings and reports and can import room data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The product is a React 18, TypeScript, and Tailwind web application. It currently uses browser local storage mock data; no backend/API server is included. Preserve existing search, map, listing, booking, comparison, account, landlord, and admin behavior during visual redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md documents the target users and product workflows. The repository includes demo listings and an existing logo/photo asset. No customer testimonials or verified market statistics are established; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make total housing costs easy to understand.
- Help renters contact landlords directly.
- Keep local search useful for Thái Nguyên neighborhoods, schools, and workplaces.
- Preserve clear listing status and trust information.
- Keep renter and landlord tasks straightforward on mobile.
